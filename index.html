About Delight Inn Stays

Delight Inn Stays is a professionally managed hotel in Kondapur, Hyderabad, located in the New Hafeezpet area. We offer clean, fully furnished, air-conditioned rooms with high-speed WiFi, flat-screen TV, and daily housekeeping — ideal for corporate travelers, IT professionals, and short-term guests.

Trusted by leading companies like Aurobindo Pharma for corporate accommodation, we maintain strict hygiene and quality standards across all floors. Whether you need a daily hotel room, monthly stay, or corporate guest house in Kondapur, Delight Inn delivers the best value.

We also offer select floors for paying guest (PG) accommodation for working professionals seeking long-term stays with meals, security, and community living.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of a room at Delight Inn Stays Kondapur?

Daily room rates start from ₹999 per night. Monthly PG stays start from ₹10,000. All rooms include AC, WiFi, TV, and daily housekeeping. Corporate rates available on request.

Is Delight Inn Stays available on Airbnb?

Yes! You can book on Airbnb for flexible short-term stays. We are also listed on Booking.com. For the best direct rates, contact us via phone or WhatsApp.

Do you offer PG accommodation in Kondapur?

Yes, we offer paying guest (PG) accommodation on select floors for working professionals. PG stays include meals, WiFi, AC, security, and daily housekeeping. Monthly rates start from ₹10,000.

Is there parking available at the hotel?

Yes, we offer free parking for both two-wheelers and four-wheelers. The parking area is secured with CCTV surveillance.

Do you provide food/meals?

Yes, we provide meals for our guests. Both vegetarian and non-vegetarian options are available. PG residents get daily meals included in their package.

Do you offer corporate tie-ups?

Yes, we have corporate tie-ups with leading companies like Aurobindo Pharma. We offer dedicated floors, GST billing, bulk booking discounts, and flexible check-in/check-out for corporate guests.
