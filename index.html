About Delight Inn Stays

Your trusted hotel in Kondapur, Hyderabad

Delight Inn Stays is a professionally managed hotel in Kondapur, Hyderabad, located in the New Hafeezpet area. We offer clean, fully furnished, air-conditioned rooms with high-speed WiFi, flat-screen TV, and daily housekeeping — ideal for corporate travelers, IT professionals, and short-term guests.

Trusted by leading companies like Aurobindo Pharma for corporate accommodation, we maintain strict hygiene and quality standards across all floors. Whether you need a daily hotel room, monthly stay, or corporate guest house in Kondapur, Delight Inn delivers the best value.

We also offer select floors for paying guest (PG) accommodation for working professionals seeking long-term stays with meals, security, and community living.
